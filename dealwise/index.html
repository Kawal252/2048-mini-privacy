DealWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DealWise (the “App”) handles information. DealWise is an offline-first property deal analysis tool intended for adults.

1. Information entered in the App

You may enter property names and locations, purchase and sale assumptions, government registration values, transaction costs, loan and EMI details, rental income, operating expenses, projected returns, notes, saved deal and portfolio details, settings, and document-register titles or categories.

The document register stores metadata only. It does not attach or upload your deeds, agreements, or other document files.

2. Where information is stored

In the current version, saved property information and app settings are stored locally in the App’s storage on your device. DealWise does not provide a user account, DealWise-operated cloud database, or cross-device synchronisation. The App does not send your saved property dataset to a DealWise backend.

Local app storage is not an encrypted vault. Someone with access to your unlocked device may be able to access locally stored information. Use your device’s security features and avoid entering information you do not wish to store on that device.

3. Sharing, reports, and backups

If you choose to export or share a report, CSV, or backup file, the information in that file may leave the App and be handled by the destination you choose, such as your device’s file manager, email, messaging, or cloud-storage app. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. Share exported files only with people and services you trust.

4. Advertising, analytics, and third-party services

The current version does not include in-app advertising or third-party analytics integrations. The core property-analysis features are designed to operate without a DealWise backend. The operating system, Google Play, and any app you choose to use for sharing or storing an exported file may process information according to their own policies and settings.

5. How information is used

Information you enter is used on your device to calculate and display property acquisition costs, loan payments, rental cash flow, investment returns, comparisons, sensitivity scenarios, saved deals, and portfolio summaries, and to create exports at your request.

6. Retention and deletion

Your information remains in local storage until you delete it in the App, clear the relevant app storage, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently remove information that has not been backed up. Deleting information from the App does not delete files you previously exported or copies held by another app or device backup. Export a backup before resetting or uninstalling if you need to keep your records.

7. Children’s privacy

DealWise is a property analysis tool intended for adults and is not designed to target children. The App does not require users to create an account.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest policy version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about DealWise, contact contact.amappstudio@gmail.com.
