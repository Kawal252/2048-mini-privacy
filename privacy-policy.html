Privacy Policy for 2048 Mini

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 2048 Mini ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

Developer

App: 2048 Mini
Privacy contact: contact.amappstudio@gmail.com

Information We Collect

2048 Mini does not require you to create an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer.

The App is designed to operate offline and does not require a developer backend, cloud account, or external server to provide its core gameplay.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store gameplay information such as game state, score, or high-score information locally on your Android device so that the App can provide its gameplay features.

This locally stored information is controlled by the App on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Sharing of Information

The App does not share personal information with third parties.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of 2048 Mini does not use advertising or third-party analytics services.

Children's Privacy

2048 Mini does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the App does not require an account or collect personal information, there is no personal profile created for users.

Data Security

Because the App does not transmit personal information to the developer, there is no developer-side database of personal user information to protect. Locally stored gameplay information remains on the user's device unless the user removes the App or otherwise clears its stored data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally by the App remains on the device until it is cleared by the user, removed by the App, or deleted when the user uninstalls the App, subject to Android device behavior.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

contact.amappstudio@gmail.com

Important Note

This policy describes the current intended data practices of 2048 Mini. If a future version adds advertising, analytics, online services, account features, or other data collection, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated before that version is published.
